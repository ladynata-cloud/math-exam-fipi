## Goal

Exercise both arithmetic routes with reproducible synthetic pupils who make
different multiplication and division mistakes. Improve the actual obstacles
they expose and make another varied practice attempt readily available in
every linked trainer. Robots are test scenarios, not evidence that a real
pupil has learned or a model of a medical condition.

## Risks and rollback

Risks are changing old generated conditions, exposing answers too early,
misleading progress and confusing a finite check with ongoing practice.
Versioned generation and legacy identity fixtures protect saved work; isolated
public history does not modify canonical state. A forward corrective release
can revert presentation while retaining decoders for any new saved generation.
Do not remove a generation decoder after real attempts use it.
