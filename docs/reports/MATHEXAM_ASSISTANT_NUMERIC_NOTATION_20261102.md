## Three completed improvements

1. Proper mixed numbers and integers are valid operands in existing standalone
   fraction addition/subtraction. `1 1/2 + 1/2` equals 2, not 6; a leading minus
   negates the whole mixed number. The complete explanation adds one conversion
   step (at most five total); hints remain one step. Ordinary fractions keep
   their original denominators and existing feedback.
2. Linear equations no longer concatenate separated digits: `1 2x + 5 = 29`
   requests a notation correction without judging the attempt. Spacing around
   operators and between a coefficient and x is preserved.
3. Both answer checkers accept exact repeating decimals, including `0,(3)`,
   `0,1(6)`, `0,(9)` and signs. BigInt arithmetic avoids float comparisons.
   The existing privacy filter still rejects long numeric runs; an equivalent
   ordinary fraction is the supported alternative at the public boundary.
