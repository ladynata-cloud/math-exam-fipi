## Goal

Let a learner open every numbered exercise and every subpart of the supplied
2019 Dorofeev/Peterson grade-six first volume, see the full original condition
and drawing together with the work area, and solve complex calculations in
verified, retained steps. Provide a separately navigable practice line with
new examples and its own progress, with a safe return to the original task.
