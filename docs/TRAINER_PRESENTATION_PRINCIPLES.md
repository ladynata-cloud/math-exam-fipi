# Trainer presentation: task first, help where it is needed

Owner direction, 2026-10-21, current MathExam conversation. This supersedes
compulsory introductory lesson screens for the revised practical trainers.

- The initial page resembles the actual task or variant. Show its condition,
  essential data, primary visualization and answer controls immediately.
- A group of questions1–5 is one continuous variant. Do not make the learner
  select a lesson step merely to see another question or recover its givens.
- Tables needed throughout a variant stay mounted and available until it is
  finished. Preserve the actual editable controls, drafts and checked work.
- On a wide screen, calculations/reference tables may occupy one pane and
  questions another. On a narrow screen, use readable stacked regions, clear
  navigation and explicit instructions for horizontally scrollable tables.
- Explanations are optional and contextual. Place questions such as «Помочь?»,
  «Что это означает?», «Как читать таблицу?» or «Как найти высоту боковины?»
  beside the relevant text, quantity or action. Further help can be nested.
- Essential givens are never treated as hints. The main visualization is not
  hidden behind a help button. Additional experiments and detailed derivations
  can be opened when useful.
- Every input tells the learner what to find and in which units. Help must
  preserve their work, allow a clear return, and never award automatic credit.
- Keep original conditions, mathematical meaning, answer validation and stored
  progress identities. Show readable numbers and use precise school terminology.

The tire trainer was the first implementation of this revised presentation.
PR293 extends it to the other seven existing practical storylines, adds
contextual laboratories to the remaining existing course pages and supports
written reasoning and theory beside geometry proofs. Its task record and PR
contain the implementation scope and release evidence. A partial source set
must remain honestly labelled; a planned but absent bank is not complete.

- Optional knowledge checks are visually distinct and never block the variant.
  The tire checks use the green heading «Тест по Шине» and «Хочешь тест?» before,
  during and after the work. Opening or answering them preserves all main drafts.
- Keep help next to the condition, before a large writing workspace. A drawing
  that requires horizontal scrolling must say so; its first and last labels
  must be reachable, and an enlargement control must preserve the learner's work.
- Every proof transition needs a specific reason. Distinguish verified textbook
  statements, original explanatory paraphrases and deductions made in the task.
